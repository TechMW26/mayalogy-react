Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mayalogy ("Mayalogy", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Service") available at mayalogy.in.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you provide

  • Profile & birth details: name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, marital status, preferred guide (MAYA / Moksh), and language preference. These are required to generate your astrological chart.
  • Contact details: mobile number (used for OTP-based sign-in) and, where applicable, email address.
  • Conversation content: messages, voice input, and choices you make while interacting with the AI guide.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, operating system, IP address, approximate location (derived from IP), and language.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, features used, session duration, and error/crash logs.
  • Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies used to keep you signed in and remember your preferences.

c) Information from third parties

  • OTP delivery providers (e.g. WhatsApp / SMS gateways) confirm successful delivery of verification codes.
  • Authentication, analytics, and crash-reporting services may share aggregated usage data with us.

2. How we use your information

  • To compute your kundli, dashas, numerology, and other astrological insights.
  • To generate personalised guidance and conversations through the AI guide.
  • To verify your identity via OTP and keep your account secure.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve the Service, including fixing bugs and developing new features.
  • To send service-related notifications (login alerts, important updates, responses to support requests).
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. AI processing

To generate readings, we send relevant chart facts and your conversation context to large-language-model providers (such as Google Gemini and ElevenLabs for voice synthesis). These providers process the data on our behalf to produce a response and are contractually restricted from using your content to train their public models, in line with their enterprise terms.

Astrological insights generated by Mayalogy are interpretive and intended for guidance only. They do not constitute medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (hosting, authentication, OTP delivery, analytics, AI inference, payment processing) under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Legal authorities when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property.
  • In a business transfer (merger, acquisition, restructuring), in which case affected users will be notified.

5. Data retention

We retain your profile and reading history for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion (see Section 7), we permanently remove personal data within 7 working days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted storage at rest where supported, access controls, and least-privilege permissions on our infrastructure. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for communications or optional features.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@mayalogy.in from your registered email or include your registered mobile number for verification.

8. Children's privacy

Mayalogy is not directed to individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Mayalogy is operated from India. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your information in India and in any other country where our service providers operate.

10. Cookies & local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your guide / language preference, cache certain readings for performance, and measure aggregate usage. You may clear this data via your browser settings; doing so may sign you out and reset preferences.

11. Third-party links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the Service or by email where appropriate. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
