Frequently asked questions

I never received my OTP. What should I do?
Please wait 60 seconds and tap Resend. If it still doesn't arrive, double-check the country code and that WhatsApp / SMS is active on the number. If the issue persists, email us with your phone number (including country code) and we'll help you sign in.
My birth time is unknown. Can I still get a reading?
Yes. Choose "I don't know my birth time" when prompted. Your reading will use a noon-based chart with reduced precision on house-based predictions, but the core analysis (signs, planets, dashas, numerology) remains accurate.
How accurate is the AI guide (MAYA / Moksh)?
Mayalogy combines traditional Vedic calculations (kundli, dashas, karakas, lal-kitab) with an AI guide that interprets your chart in plain language. Astrological insights are interpretive in nature and should be treated as guidance -not as professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.
Can I change my name, date of birth, or birth place after signing up?
Yes. Open the app, go to your profile, and edit your details. Your chart and ongoing reading will be regenerated against the new information.
How do I delete my account and data?
Send a deletion request to support@mayalogy.in from your registered phone's email or include your registered mobile number. We will permanently remove your profile, chart data, and saved readings within 7 working days.
Do you offer refunds?
Because reading content is generated and delivered instantly, we generally do not offer refunds once a reading has been opened. If you faced a technical failure (no reading delivered, double charge, etc.), email us within 7 days and we will investigate and refund eligible cases.
The app feels stuck or the voice doesn't play. What now?
Try the following in order: (1) refresh the page, (2) check that your device isn't on silent mode, (3) ensure you've granted audio permission, (4) try a different browser (Chrome / Safari latest), (5) clear site data and sign in again. If the issue persists, contact us with your device + browser details.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes. Your birth details and chat content are stored securely and used only to generate your reading. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
